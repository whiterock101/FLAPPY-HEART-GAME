# 💗 Pure Heart — Avoid the Sins

A faith-based, Flappy Bird-style game made for kids. Guide a little heart with wings and a halo through the sky, dodging thorny temptations. Bump one, and the heart turns sick and loses its wings and halo — but there's always a second chance: drag it to **confession** before time runs out to be healed and keep flying.

*To God be the glory! – Maria Lea*

## 🎮 How to Play

- **Tap, click, or press Space** to make the heart rise
- Dodge thorny branches labeled with sins — from Pride and Anger to everyday temptations like Lying, Gossip, and Bullying
- If you bump one, the heart gets sick — **drag it** to the glowing cross before the timer runs out
- Reach confession in time and you're healed — tap again to keep flying
- Watch for **✨ Grace** — sometimes God protects you before you even bump a sin. When it happens, the game explains what grace is and why you received it
- **Level up every 25 points** — new, more relatable sins are introduced, the game gets a little faster, and your halo and wings grow more radiant as a reward
- Your score is saved automatically, so you can close the game and continue later from the same spot

## 🕹 Features

- Kid-friendly, faith-based twist on a classic arcade mechanic
- Wings and a halo that visibly grow and brighten as you level up
- A "Grace" mechanic that teaches — not just protects
- Confession mechanic that gives real second chances instead of instant game-overs
- Synthesized sound effects (no external audio files needed)
- Random pillar colors and thorny obstacle designs for visual variety
- Auto-saving progress and best score
